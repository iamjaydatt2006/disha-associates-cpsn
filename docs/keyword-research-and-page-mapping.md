## 2. Audience & intent model

Four distinct searchers reach a model-making studio. Each needs a different page.

| Searcher | Who they are | Intent | Example query |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commercial buyer** | Architect, builder, developer, builder's marketing team | Transactional / commercial | "architectural model maker", "scale model maker near me" |
| **Project-type researcher** | Someone with a specific building type | Commercial investigation | "residential building model", "township model maker" |
| **Learner** | Student, junior architect, curious homeowner | Informational | "what is an architectural scale model", "model scale 1:100 meaning" |
| **Local navigator** | Someone nearby, ready to visit/call | Navigational / local | "model making near me", "Disha Associates" |

The site's job is to (a) win the **local + service intent** queries with service pages, (b) capture informational traffic with guides, and (c) convert both toward the contact page.

## 7. What is explicitly NOT targeted

- National/generic head terms ("architectural model", "3d model") — too broad, wrong intent, unwinnable without authority.
- Keyword permutations/doorway pages ("model maker in {every city}") — thin, spammy, brand-damaging.
- Unsupported claims ("best model maker in India") — unverifiable, against brief rules.
